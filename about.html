About EG Groups

Global wholesale trading and agricultural equipment solutions. Istanbul-based, with worldwide reach.

Wholesale trading with direct sourcing and quality assurance.

EG Groups is an Istanbul-based company founded in 2023, registered with Turkish Chamber of Commerce (Ticaret Odası). We specialize in wholesale trade across 48+ product categories: agricultural machinery, food & beverage export, raw materials, industrial supplies, consulting, and logistics services.

Our mission is seamless global trade — connecting importers and distributors worldwide to quality products at competitive wholesale prices. Every transaction is backed by quality inspection, reliable shipping, and transparent communication.

Refund & Returns Policy

Transparent terms for B2B wholesale transactions. Your satisfaction is our priority.

1. Policy Scope

This refund and returns policy applies to all wholesale transactions conducted by EG Groups, including agricultural machinery, food & beverage products, raw materials, industrial supplies, and services. All terms are subject to written agreement between EG Groups and the buyer.

2. Order Confirmation & Inspection Window

Upon order confirmation, EG Groups will:

  • Provide itemized invoice with product specifications, quantities, and agreed pricing
  • Complete quality inspection according to industry standards
  • Arrange shipping with tracking information provided
  • Allow buyer a 48-hour inspection window upon delivery to report defects or damage

3. Product Defects & Damaged Goods

For defective or damaged items:

  • Buyer must report damage/defects within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence
  • EG Groups will investigate and verify the claim
  • Confirmed defects or shipping damage will result in: replacement shipment at EG Groups' cost, or refund (per buyer preference)
  • Buyer must not resell or redistribute damaged goods while claim is pending

4. Non-Returnable Categories

The following cannot be returned or refunded:

  • Perishable goods: Fresh fruits, vegetables, frozen meat, dairy, and temperature-sensitive products (return period does not apply)
  • Custom or bulk orders: Items manufactured or packaged per buyer specification
  • Equipment in use: Machinery that has been operated or integrated into production
  • Expired or opened items: Products past manufacturer date or with broken seals (except for quality defects)

5. Return Procedure for Returnable Items

For products not listed in Section 4:

  • Buyer must contact EG Groups in writing within 14 days of delivery with reason for return
  • EG Groups will evaluate the request and notify buyer of approval status within 3 business days
  • If approved, buyer must arrange return shipping at their cost (unless damage was EG Groups' responsibility)
  • Return shipment must include all original packaging and documentation
  • Upon receipt and inspection, refund will be processed within 10 business days

6. Refund Timeline & Method

  • Approved refunds: Processed within 10 business days to original payment method
  • Bank transfer refunds: Allow 3–5 additional business days for clearing
  • No refund interest: Refunds are issued at the original agreed price; interest or investment gains are not included
  • Currency differences: Buyer is responsible for any foreign exchange fees charged by their bank

7. Shipping Cost Responsibility

  • Original shipment: Paid by EG Groups (included in quoted price)
  • Return shipping (buyer-requested): Paid by buyer
  • Return shipping (EG Groups defect/damage): Paid by EG Groups
  • Customs/import duties: Buyer is responsible for applicable duties on original and returned shipments

8. Dispute Resolution

If a dispute arises regarding refund eligibility:

  • Buyer and EG Groups will attempt to resolve in good faith within 14 days
  • Communication must be in writing via email to info@eggrouplimited.com
  • EG Groups commitment is response within 24 business hours
  • If unresolved, disputes may be escalated to mediation or applicable legal jurisdiction
